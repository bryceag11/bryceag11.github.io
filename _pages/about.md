I am a Ph.D. student at the [Electrical Computer and Systems Engineering Department](https://engineering.case.edu/electrical-computer-and-systems-engineering), [Case Western Reserve University](https://case.edu/) where I’m advised by Prof. [Peng “Edward” Wang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=4CbVWDcAAAAJ&hl=en). Previously, I received my bachelor's degree from the [Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering](https://engr.uky.edu/academics/departments/ece) at University of Kentucky.

I am currently a research assistant in the Augmented Intelligence for Smart Manufacturing Lab (AISM), advised by Peng Wang.

My research lies at the intersection of **robotic perception**, **geometry-aware learning**, and **causal modeling**. I’m interested in building systems that reason about pose, motion, and uncertainty—particularly through quaternion representations, causal inference, and generative models. I’m currently exploring **keypoint representations for robot imitation learning** and **sim2real transfer** in manipulation.
