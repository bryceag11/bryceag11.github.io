I am a Ph.D. student at the [Electrical Computer and Systems Engineering Department](https://engineering.case.edu/electrical-computer-and-systems-engineering), [Case Western Reserve University](https://case.edu/). I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. [Peng "Edward" Wang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=4CbVWDcAAAAJ&hl=en). Previously, I received my bachelor's degree from the [Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering](https://engr.uky.edu/academics/departments/ece) at University of Kentucky.

I am currently a research assistant in the Augmented Intelligence for Smart Manufacturing Lab (AISM), advised by Peng Wang.

My primary research interest is in Robotic Perception, which lies at the intersection of Embodied AI and Computer Vision. I focus on topics such as sequential modeling, geometry and topology, pose estimation, and robotic manipulation. Recently, I am particularly interested in keypoint design for imitation learning.
